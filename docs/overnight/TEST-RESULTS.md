## Session 27 - F04 task-scope leakage (closed offline, TS + Rust)

The reproduced leak: a session turn ("Order milk on Zepto") followed by an unrelated
request that happens to contain a pronoun ("Write a poem about it") inherited the WHOLE
previous scope - operate intent plus destination - which is exactly the condition that
routes and activates the browser loop. The cue law could not see the difference between
"the pronoun needs the previous task" and "the turn is its own task".

The mechanism is now one shared predicate, `carriesOwnTask` / `carries_own_task`: skip the
openers that cannot be a predicate (connectives, articles, prepositions, "only", copulas);
if the leading token is a CONTENT token - a word in none of this law's closed-class
vocabularies - and another content token follows it, the turn predicates an action this
domain's machinery cannot perform and names its own matter. It then inherits neither the
action nor the destination. Noun fragments and turns built from the domain's own
vocabulary keep carrying an ongoing site session, and an anaphoric research question keeps
the previous SUBJECT as evidence scope while staying non-operational.
